My Journey

I began with drawing as a way to express ideas freely, focusing more on intuition than intention. Over time, especially walking through the streets of New York, I started noticing design everywhere, logos, posters, signage and found myself wondering why they looked the way they did and how they were built. That curiosity shifted how I saw visuals, pushing me to think beyond aesthetics and into structure, process and intention.

My Design Approach

I’ve always been drawn to the quiet details most people overlook, the spacing, type and the way a layout feels before it’s even read. I trust the process and work iteratively, refining ideas until they feel clear and purposeful. I believe good design should speak for itself, communicating meaning through structure, balance and thoughtful execution.

My Skillset

My Goal

My goal is to continue growing as a designer by building meaningful, concept-driven work that balances clarity and creativity. I hope to keep learning through exploration and experience, creating designs that feel thoughtful, honest and genuinely connect with people.
